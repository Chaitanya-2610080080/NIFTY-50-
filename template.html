📖 AstraQuant X-2026: Two-Indicator Scalping Strategy (VWAP + RSI)

This quantitative trading model is engineered specifically for 5-minute intraday trading on high-liquidity Indian equities and ETFs (NIFTY 50, NIFTYBEES, RELIANCE, HDFCBANK, ICICIBANK, INFY, TCS, SBIN).

1. Mathematical Indicator Formulation

The strategy utilizes exactly two complementary technical indicators to generate asymmetric risk/reward setups:

  • Indicator 1: Intraday Volume-Weighted Average Price (VWAP)
    VWAP serves as the primary trend and institutional fair-value filter. It resets every morning at 09:15 IST:
    Typical_Price (TP) = (High + Low + Close) / 3 VWAP_t = Σ (TP_i * Volume_i) / Σ Volume_i [for i = 09:15 to current bar]
    For zero-volume index feeds (^NSEI), the system dynamically defaults to Time-Weighted Average Price (TWAP).
  • Indicator 2: Wilder's Smoothed Relative Strength Index (RSI)
    RSI serves as the mean-reversion timing trigger using an exponential Wilder decay (α = 1/period):
    Δ = Close_t - Close_{t-1} Gain = max(Δ, 0), Loss = max(-Δ, 0) Avg_Gain = α * Gain + (1 - α) * Prev_Avg_Gain Avg_Loss = α * Loss + (1 - α) * Prev_Avg_Loss RS = Avg_Gain / Avg_Loss RSI = 100 - (100 / (1 + RS))

2. Precise Trade Execution Rules

  • Long Setup: Close > VWAP AND Previous RSI < 35 AND Current RSI ≥ 35 (Crosses UP through oversold).
  • Short Setup: Close < VWAP AND Previous RSI > 65 AND Current RSI ≤ 65 (Crosses DOWN through overbought).
  • Execution Timing: Signal detected at candle close; execution occurs at next bar's Open with slippage buffer (0.01% per side).

3. Risk Management & Exit Hierarchy

  • Opening Noise Filter: No trade entries before 09:30 IST to avoid random opening volatility.
  • Afternoon Cutoff: No new trade entries after 14:45 IST.
  • Stop-Loss (SL): Placed at entry price ± 0.30% (or customized 0.10% - 1.00%).
  • Profit Target (TP): Placed at Stop Distance × Risk:Reward Ratio (e.g., 0.30% × 2.0 = 0.60%).
  • Time Stop: Strict 12-bar holding limit (12 × 5 min = 1 hour). If neither SL nor TP is hit, exit at market.
  • Daily Stop-Loss Cap: Trading terminates for the day on any symbol if net daily loss exceeds ₹1,500.
  • Mandatory Intraday Square-off: All open positions are liquidated at 15:15 IST.

4. Regulatory Transaction Charges & Friction Model

The strategy strictly accounts for real-world Indian brokerage and statutory taxes on every trade:

Brokerage = min(₹20, 0.03% of turnover) [both sides] STT (Securities Transaction Tax) = 0.025% on sell side NSE Exchange Turnover Charges = 0.00297% [both sides] SEBI Turnover Fees = 0.0001% [both sides] Stamp Duty = 0.003% on buy side GST = 18% on (Brokerage + Exchange Charges + SEBI Fees) Slippage = 0.01% per execution

5. Quantitative Finding: The NIFTYBEES Advantage

Single stock equities (TCS, HDFCBANK, SBIN) exhibit higher idiosyncratic volatility and friction drag. In contrast, NIFTYBEES (Nifty 50 ETF) exhibits smooth intraday mean-reversion, lower gap risk, and superior risk-adjusted Sharpe performance under the identical parameter rules.
